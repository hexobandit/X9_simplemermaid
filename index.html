What is SimpleMermaid?

A free, browser-based editor that turns plain text into diagrams.

SimpleMermaid is a free, browser-based editor for Mermaid.js diagrams. You write a few lines of plain text, and it turns them into a flowchart, sequence diagram, Gantt chart, class diagram, state diagram or entity relationship diagram, with a live preview that updates as you type.

Because a diagram is just text, you can keep it in version control, review changes in a pull request and paste it straight into documentation. There is no account to create and nothing to install: everything runs in your browser, so your diagrams stay with you.

How to create a Mermaid diagram

Four steps from blank page to shareable diagram.

  1. 1

    Open the editor

    Go to SimpleMermaid.com and click 'Start Creating' to open the diagram editor.

  2. 2

    Choose a diagram type

    Start with flowchart TD for top-down flowcharts, sequenceDiagram for sequence diagrams, or gantt for Gantt charts.

  3. 3

    Add content

    Type your diagram code in the editor. Use brackets for nodes [Like This] and arrows --> for connections.

  4. 4

    Share or export

    Click 'Share' for a shareable link, or export to PNG, SVG, PDF or Markdown.

New to the syntax? The Mermaid syntax tutorial and the beginner's guide walk through it step by step, and the editor includes 64 ready-made templates you can open and adapt.

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about the editor, sharing and privacy.

What is SimpleMermaid?

SimpleMermaid is a free online Mermaid.js diagram editor that lets you create flowcharts, sequence diagrams, Gantt charts, and more with instant sharing and export capabilities. No login required.

How do I create a flowchart online?

Use SimpleMermaid's online editor to create flowcharts instantly. Type 'flowchart TD' to start, add nodes with brackets like [Start], and connect them with arrows -->. Live preview shows your diagram in real-time.

Can I export diagrams to PNG or SVG?

Yes, SimpleMermaid allows you to export your diagrams to PNG for presentations or SVG for scalable graphics. Click the export buttons in the editor toolbar.

Is SimpleMermaid free to use?

Yes, SimpleMermaid is completely free. No registration, no limits, no watermarks. Create unlimited diagrams and share them with anyone.

Which diagram types does SimpleMermaid support?

Everything Mermaid.js supports, including flowcharts, sequence diagrams, class diagrams, state diagrams, entity relationship diagrams, Gantt charts and user journeys. The editor ships with 64 ready-made templates to start from.

Are my diagrams uploaded to a server?

No. Diagrams are rendered and stored in your browser. When you share a diagram, the whole diagram is compressed into the link itself, so there is nothing to upload and no account to create.

How do I share a Mermaid diagram?

Click Share in the editor to get a link. The diagram is encoded in the URL, so anyone who opens the link sees exactly what you made and can edit their own copy.

Can I highlight or draw on my diagram?

Yes. Press D (or click the highlighter button) in the editor to draw on top of your diagram with a highlighter or pen in six colors, with an eraser and undo/redo. Drawings stay aligned when you zoom or pan, and PNG, SVG and PDF exports include them unless you untick Include my drawings.
